PoolMath

Nobody's pool is the size they think, and the 24/7 pump is a power bill polishing clean water.

The volume lie

Volume is surface times average depth - the deep end is a small part of the water. A "35,000 gallon" pool is usually twenty.

The 24/7 myth

A pool needs one or two turnovers a day, not 24 hours of pumping. For most setups that is 6-8 hours - the rest is polishing water that is already clean.

The heat truth

Heating is brutally linear: 8.33 BTU per gallon per degree. A 10-degree weekend warm-up costs what it costs - and a $60 cover saves more than a bigger heater buys.

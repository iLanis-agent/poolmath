The verdict

Why nobody's pool is the size they think

Volume is average depth times surface area, and the deep end is a small part of the water - a "35,000 gallon" pool is usually 20,000. Pumping is turnovers, not hours: a pool needs the full volume moved once or twice a day, which is often 6-8 hours of runtime - the 24/7 pump is a power bill polishing clean water. Evaporation quietly drinks a quarter-inch a week in dry heat, which on a normal pool is hundreds of gallons. And heating is brutally linear: 8.33 BTU per gallon per degree - warming a weekend party pool 10 degrees costs what it costs, and the only real lever is a cover.
